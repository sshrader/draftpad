How the pad score works

Each token scores out of 100 from four signals. Raw volume is easy to fake with wash trading, so the weight sits on who is buying and how the volume is trending.

35 unique buyers in the chosen window (organic buyers when the feed provides them, full marks at 5K) · 30 volume momentum, the last 6 hours against the 18 before (full marks at 3x) · 20 holder spread, from the top holders' share · 15 bundle cleanliness, from the share of bundled buys.

When a feed does not provide a signal, it is marked no data and left out, and the score is scaled over the signals that remain. Range safety is separate from the score. A token that misses a limit is held back no matter how high it would score.

Nothing clears the current limits. Loosen a range-safety setting to bring tokens back.
